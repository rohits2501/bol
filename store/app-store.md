## Description

Bol turns a short spoken line into text you can edit and insert.

Say the sentence the way you would say it, including English words inside a Hindi sentence. Bol sends that recording to Sarvam Saaras, which is built for Indian languages, and shows the text on the phone. A cleanup pass drops filler words and a spoken “scratch that”.

On Android, the Bol keyboard records and inserts the text. On iPhone, dictate in Bol, then insert from the Bol keyboard. iOS does not allow a keyboard extension to use the microphone.

Bol does not keep the recording after the request finishes. There is no account in this version.
