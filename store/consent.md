# Consent copy

Shown on first launch, before the microphone turns on. Agreement is stored only as the local file `bol-consent.txt` containing `1`.

## Title

Microphone consent

## Body

Bol sends the recording to the server address below, which sends it to Sarvam in India to produce text. The server does not keep the audio after that request. You can edit or delete the transcript on this screen. Agree to turn the microphone on.

## Actions

- Agree and continue: writes the local flag and enables Speak.
- Closing the app without agreeing: the microphone stays off.

## Withdrawal

Delete the app, or delete `bol-consent.txt` from the app’s documents. The next launch asks again.
